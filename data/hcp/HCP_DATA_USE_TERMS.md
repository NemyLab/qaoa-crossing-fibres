# HCP-derived data in this folder

The files in this folder are derived from the Human Connectome Project Young Adult (HCP-YA) Open Access data.
As permitted by the WU-Minn HCP Consortium Open Access Data Use Terms, they are redistributed **under those
same terms**:
https://humanconnectome.org/study/hcp-young-adult/document/wu-minn-hcp-consortium-open-access-data-use-terms

By using these files you agree to those terms. In particular, do not attempt to establish the identity of, or
contact, any of the participants, and acknowledge the use of HCP data as required by the terms. These files are
**not** covered by the MIT License of the repository.

Contents:

* `hcp_patch_manifest.csv` - HCP-YA subject IDs and diffusion-grid patch centres of the 30-subject analysis.
* `hcp_subject_level_results.csv` - per-participant QAOA, MPS and assignment-quality summary metrics.
* `representative_103414/` - the representative patch: 50-node graph, exact solution, Heron bitstrings and 2D
  renderings used in Figures 1 and 3 (see its README).

No HCP image volumes are included.
